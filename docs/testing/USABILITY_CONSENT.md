## What this is

You have been asked to try out a website built by students for a university
assignment and to say what you think while you use it. It takes about 20
minutes.

## What you will be asked to do

Use the website to find a product, buy it, and a few similar tasks, while
saying out loud what you are thinking. There are no right answers. The website
is what is being assessed, not you.

## Nothing here is real

The shop is not a real shop. No real money moves. You will **never** be asked
for a real bank card — the card numbers you type are printed test numbers that
belong to a payment simulator. You will not be asked for your real email
address or any other personal detail; a made-up test account will be given to
you.

## What is written down

- What you did on the site, and how long tasks took
- What you said, including direct quotes
- Nothing that identifies you. You appear in the write-up as "P1", "P2" and so on

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary and unpaid
- You can skip any task without giving a reason
- You can stop the whole session at any point, and anything already written
  down will be destroyed if you ask
- You can ask, afterwards, to have your comments removed
